Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 16, 2026

Quick Snippets is designed to help users save and quickly reuse frequently used text. This Privacy Policy explains how the extension handles your information.

Information We Collect

Quick Snippets does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Local Storage

The extension stores your text snippets and preferences locally using Chrome's storage API. This information remains on your device and is used solely to provide the extension's functionality.

Data Sharing

Quick Snippets does not:

  • Collect analytics.
  • Sell user data.
  • Share user data with third parties.
  • Use user data for advertising.
  • Transmit snippet content to external servers.

Permissions

The extension requests only one permission:

  • Storage — Used to save your snippets and settings locally.

Remote Code

Quick Snippets does not download or execute remote JavaScript or WebAssembly. All executable code is packaged within the extension.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
