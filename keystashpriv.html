KeyStash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

KeyStash is a Chrome extension designed to help users work more efficiently by managing reusable text snippets, notes, and customizable macros. This Privacy Policy explains how KeyStash handles information when you use the extension.

1. Information We Store

KeyStash may store information that you voluntarily create or save within the extension, including:

  • Personal notes and saved text snippets.
  • Custom macros and their configurations.
  • Extension preferences and enabled features.
  • Other content you choose to save for your convenience.

This information is used to provide the extension's core features and maintain your preferences between browser sessions.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored by KeyStash is used to:

  • Save and retrieve your notes and reusable snippets.
  • Run macros that you configure and activate.
  • Copy saved content to your clipboard when requested.
  • Remember your settings and enabled features.
  • Provide the functionality you request when using the extension.

3. Local Storage and Data Sharing

KeyStash is designed to store your saved content locally in your browser using Chrome's storage capabilities. Unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, your saved notes, snippets, and macros are not transmitted to a KeyStash server.

KeyStash does not sell your personal information or share your saved content with third parties for advertising purposes. If future versions introduce cloud synchronization, analytics, or other data-sharing features, this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Clipboard Access

KeyStash may use clipboard permissions to support copying saved snippets and other content, and to read clipboard text when a supported feature requires it. Clipboard access should occur only as needed for the relevant functionality. Avoid copying sensitive information into the extension or clipboard unless necessary.

5. Website Access and Macros

Depending on the features you enable, KeyStash may interact with webpage content to insert snippets or execute user-configured macros. Such actions may affect the webpage you are using. Review your macros carefully before running them, particularly on pages containing sensitive information.

KeyStash does not intentionally collect webpage content for unrelated purposes. Website access is used to support the extension's features where permissions are granted.

6. Data Security and Retention

Your locally stored information remains subject to the security of your browser, device, and user account. You can remove saved content through the extension's available controls, where provided, or by removing the extension and its associated data through Chrome. Removing the extension may result in the loss of locally stored information.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and browser.

7. Children's Privacy

KeyStash is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children. The extension does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in KeyStash's functionality, permissions, or applicable requirements. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
