01 🔍 Information We Collect
✓ 2Much2Remember stores all your data locally on your device. We do not operate servers or cloud databases.

The app collects and stores the following data only on your device:

  • Bills & financial data — bill names, amounts, due dates, categories, payment URLs you enter
  • Calendar events — birthdays, appointments, reminders you create
  • To-do items — task titles, notes, priority levels, due dates
  • Scheduled messages — recipient names, phone numbers, message text, scheduled times
  • Notification preferences — reminder settings you configure

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or account credentials
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Contacts (phone numbers are entered manually by you)
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry
02 ⚙️ How We Use Your Data

All data you enter is used solely to provide the app's features:

  • Displaying your bills, events, tasks, and messages within the app
  • Scheduling local push notifications on your device
  • Opening pre-filled Messages via sms: deep links when you request it
  • Opening payment URLs in Safari when you tap a bill's payment link
Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or sold to any third party — ever.
03 💾 Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored using Apple's UserDefaults and local device storage. Data never leaves your device through our app.

  • Data is protected by your device's built-in encryption (iOS Secure Enclave)
  • If you enable iCloud Backup, Apple may back up your device data per their own privacy policy
  • Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all stored data

For iCloud Backup practices, refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

04 🔗 Third Parties & Sharing
✓ We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties.

The app interacts with the following Apple system services only:

UNUserNotificationCenter UIApplication (sms: links) Safari (payment URLs) iOS UserDefaults

These are native Apple APIs. No data is transmitted to external servers through these interactions.

05 🔔 Push Notifications

2Much2Remember uses local notifications only — no push notification servers are involved.

  • All notifications are scheduled directly on your device via iOS
  • No notification data is transmitted over the internet
  • You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings → 2Much2Remember → Notifications
  • Disabling notifications does not affect app functionality
06 🇪🇺 Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access — You can view all your data directly within the app at any time
  • Right to Rectification — You can edit any data within the app at any time
  • Right to Erasure — You can delete individual records or uninstall the app to erase all data
  • Right to Portability — Your data lives on your device; you control it
  • Right to Object — Since we process no personal data on servers, there is nothing to object to
  • Right to Restrict Processing — Disabling notifications restricts notification processing
Because all data is stored locally on your device and we have no servers, we act as neither a Data Controller nor Data Processor in the traditional GDPR sense. You are the sole controller of your own data.

Our lawful basis for any processing that occurs locally on your device is Legitimate Interest — enabling the core functionality you explicitly requested by using the app.

07 🇺🇸 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected — none is collected by us
  • Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed — it is not
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information — not applicable; we do not sell data
  • Access their personal information — all data is visible within the app
  • Equal service and price, even if they exercise their privacy rights
We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
08 👶 Children's Privacy

2Much2Remember is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all data is stored locally and no accounts are required, the app cannot distinguish users by age. Parental guidance is advised for younger users.

If you believe a child has entered personal information into the app, you can remove it by editing or deleting entries directly within the app, or uninstalling it.

09 🗂️ Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as the app is installed on your device. You are in full control:

  • Delete individual items at any time within the app
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data
  • We have no ability to retain, recover, or access your data after deletion

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
